## MSP redesign study (pre-declared 2026-10-24 19:25 KST, before any run; Selim's request via Discord 19:19)

Trigger: on test, MSP b300 trails the deterministic (direct) scorer (within-scene AUC 0.855 vs
0.876; top-1 0.650 vs 0.683, 5 seeds each) and the beta sweep is monotone (beta 1000: 0.660).

Rules: candidates are selected on the VALIDATION split only (held-out-object val as a secondary
check); the final design is frozen before any test evaluation; test is then run once with 5 seeds.
Every candidate gets a matched direct-scorer counterpart with the same extra ingredient, budget,
augmentation and selection rule (epochs >= 5, own val loss). 3 seeds per variant on val. Every
variant and its val result is logged below; nothing is discarded silently. If no candidate beats
its matched direct counterpart on val beyond seed noise (difference > 2 x pooled seed s.d.), stop
and fall back to the rate-quality story, stated plainly in the paper and the final report.

| ID | Candidate | Hypothesis | MSP variant | Matched control |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| C1 | Decision-aligned loss: add a within-scene pairwise logistic ranking loss on the (marginal) success logit, weight beta x 1 | per-candidate BCE spends capacity on per-object base rates; the decision only needs the within-scene order, so a ranking term should help most where the bottleneck limits capacity | `--method msp --rank 1` | `--method direct --rank 1` |
| C2 | Sufficiency distillation: soft success targets 0.5 y + 0.5 t, t = mean prediction of a 10-model teacher (direct seeds 5-14, never evaluated as single models) | a bottleneck trained on denoised teacher targets keeps the ensemble's decision statistic at low rate; label noise from unobservable physics is what the stochastic code currently has to absorb | `--method msp --distill 0.5` | `--method direct --distill 0.5` |
| C3 | Operational-rate code: replace the Gaussian KL by the entropy-coded length of a uniformly quantised deterministic code (additive U(-1/2,1/2) noise in training, factorised learned prior, rate in bits weighted by lambda) | the payload headline is measured in entropy-coded bits; optimising those bits directly, through the decision loss, should beat post-hoc PCA + quantisation at matched payload | `--method qcode --lam {0.01,0.03,0.1}` | direct + PCA + same quantiser/entropy coder (val) |

beta = 300 on the success NLL, the stochastic MSP pays 1 per nat (1.44 bits) of KL, so lambda
of order 1 per bit is the comparable scale; the first grid {0.01, 0.03, 0.1} would have left the
rate essentially unpenalised.
