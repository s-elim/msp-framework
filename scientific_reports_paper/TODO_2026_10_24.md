## Decisions recorded

* Training budget: 60 epochs, AdamW lr 5e-4 (pose/MSP/direct), Adam 1e-3 (GQ-CNN, as Dex-Net), batch 64, one GPU each.
* Slurm needs `--mem` (teammate session confirmed with the admin via Selim).
* Gripper: 140 mm class stroke, 7 cm fingers, 10 N per finger; physics prior friction
  LogN(log 0.5, 0.4), mass = mesh volume x 500 kg/m^3 x LogN(0, 0.4), COM ~ N(0, (0.15 h)^2).
  Chosen after pilot 1 (30 N, 5 cm fingers) gave 2-18 % executable grasps and ~90 % success
  when executable, which is uninformative. LM masses are not published, so this is a prior.
* Inexecutable candidates (open jaws already in contact) count as failures in every metric.
* 13 objects (standard LM protocol; bowl and cup excluded). Real test images only; the
  rendered `train` split is not used. Crops use the GT visible-mask bounding box (the usual
  "known detection" assumption), stated in the paper.
