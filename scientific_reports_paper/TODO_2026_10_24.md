| ID | Candidate | Hypothesis | MSP variant | Matched control |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| C1 | Decision-aligned loss: add a within-scene pairwise logistic ranking loss on the (marginal) success logit, weight beta x 1 | per-candidate BCE spends capacity on per-object base rates; the decision only needs the within-scene order, so a ranking term should help most where the bottleneck limits capacity | `--method msp --rank 1` | `--method direct --rank 1` |
| C2 | Sufficiency distillation: soft success targets 0.5 y + 0.5 t, t = mean prediction of a 10-model teacher (direct seeds 5-14, never evaluated as single models) | a bottleneck trained on denoised teacher targets keeps the ensemble's decision statistic at low rate; label noise from unobservable physics is what the stochastic code currently has to absorb | `--method msp --distill 0.5` | `--method direct --distill 0.5` |
| C3 | Operational-rate code: replace the Gaussian KL by the entropy-coded length of a uniformly quantised deterministic code (additive U(-1/2,1/2) noise in training, factorised learned prior, rate in bits weighted by lambda) | the payload headline is measured in entropy-coded bits; optimising those bits directly, through the decision loss, should beat post-hoc PCA + quantisation at matched payload | `--method qcode --lam {0.01,0.03,0.1}` | direct + PCA + same quantiser/entropy coder (val) |

## Study 3 (pre-declared 2026-10-24 23:00 KST, before any VLM call): a VLM as a training-time outcome teacher

Motivation from studies 1-2 and the FP analysis: (i) the bottleneck does not help but a teacher
does (C2); (ii) the C2 teacher is an ensemble trained on every object, so it cannot be used in
the held-out-object protocol, where learned scorers fall furthest (direct 0.822 within-AUC vs

### Study 4 (pre-declared before any run): pose-uncertainty-marginalised planning

Observation motivating it (test, already reported): FP + planner 0.791 top-1 vs exact pose 0.856;
FP Sel@25% 0.902 vs ensemble 0.938. Both gaps are what a planner that trusts a point estimate
should show: candidates near an edge are scored as if the pose were exact, and the planner's
confidence ignores pose error. FoundationPose returns no covariance, so a pose posterior has to
be built from what it does return.

Uncertainty model U (no FoundationPose retraining, no new GPU run): residuals of the FP estimate
against the annotated pose on the TRAIN split, rotation residual in the object frame
(dR = R_hat^T R_gt, reduced over the BOP discrete symmetries of eggbox and glue) and translation
residual in the camera frame (dt = t_gt - t_hat). A scene's posterior is a bootstrap over the
residuals of its k = 64 nearest train scenes of the same object in a space of observable fit
features computed from the estimated pose, the mask and the depth image only (median and 90th
percentile point-to-model distance of the visible points, outlier fraction, silhouette overlap,
mask spill outside the silhouette, valid-depth fraction, log mask area, and the viewing direction
in the estimated object frame, so neighbours share a viewpoint and object-frame rotation
residuals transfer; added 01:10 KST, before any run). No scene draws residuals from its own
object's frames within one 50-frame block of it, in ANY split (cross-fitting for train; for
val/calib/test it removes the near-duplicate frames on the other side of a split boundary), so
neither the POISE-U training inputs nor the evaluation posteriors are leaked.

Planner: M = 16 pose samples x P = 4 physics draws (common random numbers across poses and
candidates), score = posterior mean success, plus per-candidate executable probability and the
25 % quantile over poses (logged as rejection evidence, not used for ranking).

| ID | Variant | Hypothesis | Controls |
|---|---|---|---|
| U1 | residual posterior, feature-conditioned (kNN) | marginalising over a calibrated pose posterior ranks pose-robust candidates first and lowers the planner's confidence where the pose is unreliable: higher top-1 and Sel@25% than the point estimate | (a) point estimate with 64 physics draws (same 1024 rollouts per scene: rules out Monte Carlo variance); (b) isotropic Gaussian perturbation with each object's marginal residual scale (rules out generic smoothing); (c) unconditional bootstrap over the object's residuals (tests the feature conditioning) |
| POISE-U | POISE with the U1 planner score as the action feature | the learned head inherits U1's pose robustness | POISE (fpose), same recipe, 5 seeds |

Calibration check of U before planning (val): coverage of the 50/80/90 % posterior quantiles of
rotation angle and translation norm error. Selection (val only): top-1, within-scene AUC,

### Study 5 (pre-declared, runs only if U1 is kept): triggered second view

the empirical residual shape (bootstrap >> moment-matched Gaussian); the feature conditioning's
val advantage over the unconditional bootstrap did NOT replicate on test (+0.002 n.s.), and
residuals from other objects keep ~90 % of the gain. With an uncertainty-aware planner the learned
head (POISE-U) adds nothing over its planner input; the stochastic bottleneck adds nothing anywhere.
