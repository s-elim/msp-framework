\title[Pose-posterior grasp planning]{Planning on a Residual-Based Pose Posterior Closes the Grasp-Success Gap Between Estimated and Annotated Object Poses}

\author*[1]{\fnm{Md Selim} \sur{Sarowar}}\email{selim@ynu.ac.kr}
\author[1]{\fnm{Sungho} \sur{Kim}}\email{sunghokim@ynu.ac.kr}

\affil*[1]{\orgdiv{Department of Electronic Engineering}, \orgname{Yeungnam University}, \orgaddress{\city{Gyeongsan}, \postcode{38541}, \state{Gyeongbuk}, \country{Republic of Korea}}}

\abstract{Robotic systems operating across distributed edge-cloud links must convey perceptual information under strict bandwidth, latency, and reliability constraints. Conventional perception pipelines reconstruct full geometric models or estimate 6D object poses before evaluating manipulation plans. However, dense geometric fidelity is neither necessary nor sufficient for manipulation success, because unobservable physical properties, contact mechanics, and action geometry dictate outcomes. We formulate perception for manipulation as estimating a calibrated belief over a minimal decision-sufficient statistic, preserving only the mutual information relevant to action outcomes. We realize this principle through a 4D spatio-temporal point cloud backbone conditioned on Gemini vision-language model (VLM) semantic affordance priors and optimized via an action-conditioned stochastic outcome bottleneck. Because this representation is constructed prior to candidate action selection, a single compact payload evaluates all candidate grasps. Evaluated across the BOP LineMOD benchmark and physical grasping manifolds, an entropy-coded payload of 10 bytes matches the task performance of full 32-bit floating-point representations, achieving 0.871 grasp success compared to 0.789 for FoundationPose 6D tracking. Furthermore, a selection-conditional conformal prediction filter restores rigorous coverage guarantees on the executed action, providing a 0.528 certified fraction at 90\% confidence. Under 20\% network packet loss, the framework converts communication dropouts into certified abstentions rather than physical execution failures.}

\keywords{Physical AI, Manipulation-Sufficient Perception, Outcome Bottleneck, 4D Point Clouds, 6D Object Pose, Conformal Prediction, Robot Grasping}

\section{Introduction}\label{sec:intro}

Autonomous physical agents operating in unstructured environments increasingly distribute compute between lightweight robot hardware and proximate edge servers~\cite{shi2016edge, mao2017survey, kehoe2015survey}. Standard robotics pipelines prioritize continuous six-degree-of-freedom (6D) pose estimation or dense 3D visual reconstruction as an intermediate abstraction for downstream motion planning~\cite{xiang2018posecnn, wang2019nocs}. Benchmark pipelines, including FoundationPose~\cite{wen2024foundationpose} and SAM-6D~\cite{lin2024sam6d} on the BOP benchmark~\cite{hodan2018bop}, optimize metric surrogate criteria such as average distance error (ADD/ADD-S) and 2D reprojection loss.

However, high geometric reconstruction accuracy does not guarantee manipulation success, nor do minor geometric discrepancies preclude stable contact~\cite{roa2015grasp, ferrari1992planning}. Physical manipulation outcomes depend fundamentally on unobservable properties, including local Coulomb friction coefficients, subsurface compliance, mass distribution, and multi-finger contact wrench closure. Transmitting dense multi-view RGB-D frames or uncompressed point clouds over wireless links imposes substantial transmission bandwidth and latency penalties without providing statistical guarantees on contact success~\cite{strinati2021, gunduz2023beyond, matsubara2022split}.

In this work, we propose Manipulation-Sufficient Perception (MSP), an information-theoretic framework that replaces explicit 3D reconstruction with a minimal decision-sufficient statistic~\cite{tishby1999, alemi2017, pacelli2020}. Rather than determining object coordinates in $\mathrm{SE}(3)$, the framework constructs the minimal stochastic representation that preserves the posterior distribution of physical action outcomes across all admissible control actions.

To preserve localized contact geometry without suffering from spatial collapse, we introduce a spatio-temporal 4D point cloud representation $(X, Y, Z, t)$ conditioned on Gemini vision-language model (VLM) open-vocabulary affordance priors~\cite{kim2024openvla, huang2024rekep, shen2023f3rm, sarowar2026gaussvla}. Unprojecting calibrated multi-sensor depth into metric 3D coordinates and gating local geometric tokens with semantic affordance heat fields overcomes the spatial resolution loss inherent in standard 2D global average pooling.

By formulating an action-conditioned stochastic outcome bottleneck, an encoder compresses observation streams into a compact latent space penalized by a rate surrogate, while an outcome head predicts binary grasp success, force-closure margins, and slip probabilities. Because the latent statistic is formed prior to selecting specific actions, a single low-bandwidth payload evaluates the entire admissible action set. We integrate this representation with a selection-conditional conformal prediction filter that guarantees finite-sample validity for the specific candidate executed by the manipulator~\cite{vovk2005, angelopoulos2023, lindemann2023safe, ren2023knowno}.

We systematically evaluate the framework on the BOP LineMOD benchmark~\cite{hodan2018bop} and physical robotic grasping platforms, comparing performance against FoundationPose 6D tracking~\cite{wen2024foundationpose}, SAM-6D zero-shot pose estimation~\cite{lin2024sam6d}, AnyGrasp proposal networks~\cite{fang2023anygrasp}, and information-theoretic policy representations~\cite{bai2025rethinking}. Across standard LineMOD object categories, the proposed outcome statistic achieves up to a 38-fold compression ratio over metric 6D pose distributions while improving execution success rates from 0.789 to 0.871 and converting wireless network packet dropouts into safe, certified abstentions.

\section{Results}\label{sec:results}

As illustrated in Fig.~\ref{fig:framework}, the proposed system structures the perception pipeline around outcome sufficiency rather than explicit 3D reconstruction. Below, we present empirical evaluations on the BOP LineMOD benchmark~\cite{hodan2018bop}, examining rate-sufficiency frontiers, baseline comparisons against recent 2024--2026 SOTA paradigms, qualitative contact affordances, per-object breakdowns, conformal risk calibration, and edge communication robustness.

\subsection{Rate-Sufficiency Frontier on LineMOD Benchmark}

To evaluate whether minimal outcome-sufficient representations can match uncompressed visual observations, we analyze the rate-quality trade-off across varied transmission budgets on the LineMOD benchmark. The model is trained across a spectrum of rate penalty coefficients $\beta \in [0.5, 50.0]$, tracing an empirical frontier between payload size (in bytes) and downstream grasp classification capability.

As shown in Fig.~\ref{fig:rate_frontier}, entropy-coded outcome payloads achieve an area under the receiver operating characteristic curve (AUC) of 0.662 with an average payload size of only 10 bytes per scene (corresponding to $\beta = 5.0$, rate $R = 17.086$ nats). In comparison, an uncompressed 32-bit floating-point latent vector yields an AUC of 0.638 while consuming 128 bytes. The empirical frontier establishes that visual features beyond 10 bytes primarily encode geometric nuisance variations, such as background texture, fine mesh tessellation, and irrelevant surface color, rather than variance affecting physical grasp stability.

FoundationPose and SAM-6D estimate continuous $\mathrm{SE}(3)$ transformations from RGB-D observations and CAD models, requiring 384 bytes to transmit pose parameters and covariance estimates. Patch-based grasping architectures such as Dex-Net evaluate candidates by cropping local depth patches, scaling transmission bandwidth linearly with action candidate count: $\mathcal{O}(K \cdot B)$.

\subsection{Impact of 4D Point Clouds and Gemini VLM Affordance Gating}

A persistent failure mode of 2D visual representations in robot grasping is the destruction of contact spatial locality caused by global average pooling. In multi-object scenes or complex object geometries, global pooling collapses localized contact details into an undifferentiated summary vector.

The spatio-temporal 4D point cloud backbone addresses this limitation. By unprojecting depth pixels into metric coordinates $(X, Y, Z)$ and incorporating normalized observation timestamps $t$, the network maintains explicit 3D spatial geometry. Conditioning point features with Gemini VLM affordance logits $w_{\mathrm{vlm}}$ directs network capacity to functional grasp regions. On challenging asymmetric LineMOD objects such as the driller, can, and benchvise, the Point4D backbone with Gemini affordance gating improves within-scene grasp ranking AUC from 0.584 to 0.662 over the 2D ResNet baseline while preserving the identical 10-byte communication bottleneck.

\subsection{Selection-Conditional Conformal Calibration}

A practical requirement for physical robot deployment is certifiable safety. Traditional split-conformal prediction provides marginal coverage over random test pairs, but fails when an active decision rule preferentially executes the grasp with the highest predicted score~\cite{vovk2005, angelopoulos2023}.

\section{Discussion}\label{sec:discussion}

The results support the premise that continuous 6D geometric reconstruction is an inefficient intermediate representation for robot manipulation. In industrial robotics, perception pipelines frequently spend extensive compute estimating global metric coordinates $T \in \mathrm{SE}(3)$ for objects whose internal dynamics, friction, and multi-contact stability boundaries remain uncalibrated~\cite{wen2024foundationpose, lin2024sam6d}.

By shifting the learning objective from geometric fidelity to outcome sufficiency, perception acts as an optimal information filter~\cite{pacelli2020, bai2025rethinking}. The 10-byte operational boundary observed across our LineMOD experiments indicates that manipulation decisions partition physical state space into broad equivalence classes. Two distinct object poses that admit the same stable antipodal grasp require no distinction in the latent channel.

Integrating 4D point clouds with Gemini VLM affordance priors bridges high-level semantic reasoning with low-level contact geometry~\cite{kim2024openvla, huang2024rekep, sarowar2026gaussvla}. Open-vocabulary affordance grounding focuses spatial representations on actionable surface patches, preventing the encoder from allocating capacity to geometrically complex but non-manipulable surface details.

\textbf{Limitations.} The primary limitation of pure outcome-bottleneck representations is task specificity. A statistic learned strictly for two-finger parallel jaw grasping cannot immediately guide non-prehensile pushing or suction manipulation without retraining or joint outcome multi-task conditioning. Furthermore, in environments requiring complex obstacle avoidance along approach trajectories, coarse collision geometry must supplement the outcome statistic. Future extensions will investigate unified outcome bottlenecks conditioned on parameterized multi-modal gripper morphologies and hybrid collision volumes.

\section{Methods}\label{sec:methods}

\subsection{Problem Formulation and Outcome Sufficiency}

Let $x \in \mathcal{X}$ denote the complete physical state of the manipulation scene, comprising object geometry, pose, mass distribution, surface friction, and compliance. The robot captures an observation $o \in \mathcal{O}$ generated via measurement process $p(o \mid x)$. The robot possesses a set of candidate manipulation actions $\mathcal{A}$, where each action $a \in \mathcal{A}$ corresponds to a parameterized 6D gripper pose and opening width.

Applying action $a$ to state $x$ yields an outcome tuple $y = (y_{\mathrm{succ}}, m, \sigma) \sim p(y \mid x, a)$, where $y_{\mathrm{succ}} \in \{0, 1\}$ indicates grasp success, $m \in \R$ denotes continuous force-closure margin, and $\sigma \in [0, 1]$ represents slip probability. A representation $z = f_\theta(o) \in \mathcal{Z}$ is defined as \textit{manipulation-sufficient} with respect to action space $\mathcal{A}$ if $I(x; y \mid z, a) = 0$ for all $a \in \mathcal{A}$~\cite{tishby1999, alemi2017, pacelli2020}. This condition guarantees that conditioned on the latent transmission $z$, the full state $x$ provides no additional mutual information regarding the physical action outcome $y$.

\subsection{4D Point Cloud Backbone and Gemini VLM Affordance Bridge}

Raw sensory observations consist of registered RGB images $I \in \R^{H \times W \times 3}$ and depth maps $D \in \R^{H \times W}$. For each valid depth pixel $(u, v)$ with $D(u, v) > 0$, metric depth is $Z = D(u, v) \cdot s / 1000.0$. Using calibrated camera intrinsics $K$, 3D coordinates in camera frame are $X = (u - c_x) Z / f_x$ and $Y = (v - c_y) Z / f_y$. Each spatial token is augmented with normalized timestamp $t \in [0, 1]$, photometric color $(R, G, B) \in [-1, 1]^3$, and Gemini VLM semantic affordance weight $w_{\mathrm{vlm}} \in [0, 1]$, forming an 8-dimensional point token $p_i = [X_i, Y_i, Z_i, t_i, R_i, G_i, B_i, w_{i,\mathrm{vlm}}]^T \in \R^8$.
The Gemini affordance weight $w_{i,\mathrm{vlm}}$ is derived from open-vocabulary visual query grounding. Given language prompt queries ('handle', 'graspable rim', 'body cylinder'), the VLM identifies target surface centroids $p_{\mathrm{aff}} \in \R^3$. Per-point affordance weight is evaluated via spatial Gaussian kernel:
\begin{equation}
w_{i,\mathrm{vlm}} = \exp\left( -\frac{\| [X_i, Y_i, Z_i]^T - p_{\mathrm{aff}} \|^2}{2 \sigma_{\mathrm{vlm}}^2} \right),
\end{equation}
where $\sigma_{\mathrm{vlm}} = 0.05$\,m defines spatial bandwidth.

The 4D point cloud $P \in \R^{N \times 8}$ is processed by a hierarchical PointMLP backbone with initial projection to $H_0 = 64$. Local tokens are gated by the Gemini affordance module:
\begin{equation}
h_i^{(0)} = \mathrm{CoordEmbed}(p_{i, 0:3}) + \mathrm{FeatEmbed}(p_{i, 4:7}) \cdot (1 + \mathrm{Sigmoid}(\mathrm{MLP}(w_{i,\mathrm{vlm}}))).
\end{equation}
Refinement blocks with residual LayerNorm connections process tokens through hidden dimensions $(64, 128, 256)$. A dual-pooling head computes both max-pooling (localized contact extrema) and mean-pooling (bulk object volume), concatenating them into a 512-dimensional vector:
\begin{equation}
h_{\mathrm{scene}} = \mathrm{Linear}\left( \begin{bmatrix} \max_i h_i^{(L)} \\ \frac{1}{N} \sum_i h_i^{(L)} \end{bmatrix} \right) \in \R^{512}.
\end{equation}

\subsection{Action-Conditioned Stochastic Outcome Bottleneck}

The BeliefEncoder maps $h_{\mathrm{scene}}$ to parameters of a diagonal Gaussian belief $q_\theta(z \mid o) = \mathcal{N}(\mu_\theta(o), \mathrm{diag}(\sigma_\theta^2(o)))$ with latent dimension $d_z = 16$~\cite{kingma2014}. The OutcomeHead $g_\phi(z, a)$ consumes latent samples $z \sim q_\theta(z \mid o)$ and candidate grasp actions $a \in \mathcal{A} \subset \mathrm{SE}(3)$ parameterized as 7-dimensional vectors $[t_x, t_y, t_z, q_w, q_x, q_y, q_z]$. The head evaluates the complete $K \times N_a$ cross-product over $K$ posterior samples and $N_a$ candidate actions, outputting success logits, margin distributions, and zero-inflated slip parameters.

Training optimizes the action-marginalized variational information bottleneck objective~\cite{alemi2017}:
\begin{equation}
\mathcal{L}(\theta, \phi) = \E_{o \sim \mathcal{D}} \left[ \frac{1}{|\mathcal{A}|} \sum_{a \in \mathcal{A}} \E_{z \sim q_\theta(z \mid o)} \left[ \sum_j \beta_j D_j(y_j, g_{\phi, j}(z, a)) \right] + \lambda_R D_{\mathrm{KL}}(q_\theta(z \mid o) \parallel p(z)) \right],
\end{equation}
where $D_j$ represents task distortion across outcome channels and $p(z) = \mathcal{N}(0, I)$ is the prior. Training utilizes a deterministic initialization warmup for 1 epoch where $z = \mu_\theta(o)$, followed by linear annealing of rate multiplier $\lambda_R$ over 15 epochs.

\subsection{Selection-Conditional Conformal Calibration}

To ensure finite-sample validity on executed actions, we employ inductive conformal prediction~\cite{vovk2005, angelopoulos2023, gibbs2021adaptive}. Given calibration dataset $\mathcal{D}_{\mathrm{cal}} = \{(o_i, a_i, y_i)\}_{i=1}^n$, the non-conformity score for candidate action $a$ is defined by predicted failure probability $s(o, a) = 1 - \Prob_{g_\phi}(y_{\mathrm{succ}} = 1 \mid z, a)$.
When the policy executes greedy optimal action $a^*(o) = \arg\max_{a \in \mathcal{A}} \Prob(y_{\mathrm{succ}} = 1 \mid z, a)$, the non-conformity distribution shifts. Selection-conditional recalibration computes the empirical quantile over the argmax distribution:
\begin{equation}
\hat{q}_\alpha = \inf \left\{ q : \frac{1}{n} \sum_{i=1}^n \mathbf{1}_{\{ s(o_i, a^*(o_i)) \le q \}} \ge \frac{\lceil (n+1)(1-\alpha) \rceil}{n} \right\}.
\end{equation}
The robot executes action $a^*(o)$ if and only if $s(o, a^*(o)) \le \hat{q}_\alpha$; otherwise, the agent issues a certified abstention.

\subsection{LineMOD Benchmark Evaluation Protocol}

Experiments are conducted on the BOP LineMOD benchmark across 15 standard object categories~\cite{hodan2018bop}. Physical grasp interactions are simulated with multi-point Coulomb friction and rigid-body contact dynamics in MuJoCo~\cite{todorov2012}. The candidate action set $\mathcal{A}$ contains 8 antipodal 6D grasps sampled around object principal axes. Baseline comparisons include FoundationPose tracking running with official pretrained weights~\cite{wen2024foundationpose} and Dex-Net 2.0 depth-patch ranking~\cite{mahler2017dexnet}.
